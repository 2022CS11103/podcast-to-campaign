FAQ

Before you subscribe

Does this publish to my accounts automatically?

No. The calendar is a plan. YouTube upload exists when you connect it; Instagram, LinkedIn, and X can be connected for later publishing. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

What can I upload?

A public YouTube or podcast URL, or an MP4 / MOV / MKV / WebM file. Long-form talks, interviews, and episodes work best — that’s what the editor is built for.

Do I have to connect social accounts?

No. You can generate and download a complete campaign without connecting a channel. Connect later when you are ready to publish.

How does the trial work?

New subscriptions include 7 days free. Cancel in Stripe before the trial ends and you will not be charged. After that, billing follows the monthly or yearly plan you picked.
