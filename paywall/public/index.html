FAQ

Before you subscribe

Does this publish to my accounts automatically?

No. The calendar is a plan. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

What can I upload?

A public YouTube or podcast URL, or an MP4 / MOV / MKV / WebM file. Long-form talks and interviews work best.

Do I have to connect social accounts?

No. Generate and download a full campaign without connecting. Connect later when you’re ready to publish.

How does the trial work?

New subscriptions include 7 days free. Cancel in Stripe before the trial ends and you won’t be charged.
